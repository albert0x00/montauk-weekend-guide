Montauk Weekend Guide 2026

Last updated: October 2026 · Reference code: MTK-2026-GUIDE-77431 · A practical planning guide for weekend travelers visiting Montauk, New York.

Overview

Montauk, at the eastern tip of Long Island, is one of New York's most popular weekend destinations. Expect oceanfront hotels, lighthouse walks, whale-watching tours (seasonal), and a small-town atmosphere that fills up fast on summer weekends. Mid-October weekends are quieter, with rates typically between $160 and $350 per night for standard ocean-view rooms.

Book at least three weeks ahead for holiday weekends. Non-refundable rates are common at the lower tiers and can save 8–12%.

Things to Do

  • Montauk Point Lighthouse — the iconic 1797 lighthouse, open seasonally; the cliff walk around it takes about 45 minutes.
  • Vineyards — Montauk is one of the top wine regions on Long Island; most cellars offer tastings without appointments.
  • Fishers Island ferry — seasonal day trips from Montauk Harbor.
  • Beaches — Main Beach and Dune Road Beach are the two most accessible public beaches.
  • Dining — seafood shacks on Main Street; book tables for Friday/Saturday dinner.

Local Events (October 2026)

  • First weekend of October: fall farmers market on Main Street
  • Mid-October: lighthouse heritage walk (guided, free)
  • Late October: harvest wine festival at the Montauk vineyard corridor

Planning Checklist

  • Confirm hotel cancellation policy before booking non-refundable rates
  • Check ferry schedules if planning a Fishers Island day trip
  • Pack layers — October evenings on the point drop to the mid-40s°F
  • Reserve dinner for at least one night in advance
